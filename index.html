About

    

      

        
I'm an MPhil student at the UCL Knowledge Lab, University College London, working with Professor Mutlu Cukurova. Previously, I completed my M.S. at the Stanford Graduate School of Education, working with Associate Professor Benjamin Domingue.

        
I study the potential of large language models for student simulation combined with psychometrics, spanning applications in test design, teacher training and personalized learning. I extend this work to measuring and developing teachers' competency in AI, finding ways to help teachers navigate their experiences with rapidly advancing technologies.

        
I focus on the intersection of AI, education and psychometrics to achieve the simple, yet difficult, goal of education: helping students prepare for emerging opportunities in a world shaped by research, technology and AI.
